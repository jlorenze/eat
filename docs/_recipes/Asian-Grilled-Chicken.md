pair:
- rice

ingredients:
- 3 lbs boneless chicken breast or thighs
- 4 Tbsp lemon juice
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 tsp fresh ginger, minced
- 4 green onions, minced
- 1/2 cup low sodium soy sauce
- 1/2 cup teriyaki sauce
- 2 tsp honey
- 4 tsp sesame oil
- 4 Tbsp toasted sesame seeds
directions:
- If using chicken breast, slice in half into cutlets
- Combine all marinade ingredients (lemon juice - sesame oil), evenly cover chicken
- Marinate in refrigerator for 1-24 hours
- Grill 5-8 minutes per side until done
- Top with sesame seeds
