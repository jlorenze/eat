ingredients:
- 2 lbs ground beef
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup plain breadcrumbs
- 1/2 cup parmesan cheese
- 1/2 cup minced yellow onion
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp Italian seasonings
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1/4 cup sliced basil
- 48 oz tomato sauce
directions:
- Set oven rack to lower-middle, set to broil. Line baking sheet with foil and lightly grease with olive oil
- Mix beef, eggs, breadcrumbs, cheese, onion, salt, pepper, seasoning, garlic, and basil
- Make meatballs with 1/3 cup mixture and evenly space on sheet
- Broil meatballs 12-15 minutes, turning part way through, to brown
- Heat sauce in pot with lid, add broiled meatballs to sauce, cover and simmer on low heat for at least 1.5 hours

notes:
- Can also just bake meatballs at 375F for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway through
- Can also cook broiled meatballs in slow cooker with sauce added for 3 hours on high or 6 on low
- Can freeze cooked meatballs and sauce for 1 month
