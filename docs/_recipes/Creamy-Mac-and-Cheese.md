tags:

ingredients:
- 8oz uncooked pasta
- 2 Tbsp salted butter
- 2 Tbsp AP flour
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1/4 cup sour cream or Greek yogurt
- 8 oz shredded cheddar cheese
directions:
- Cook pasta according to package, save some pasta water. Drain and set aside
- Mix flour, salt, garlic powder in small bowl
- In medium saucepan, melt butter over medium heat
- Add flour mixture and whisk until smooth
- Add milk and whisk until smooth
- Add sour cream or yogurt, whisk until smooth
- Cook on medium-high heat until thickened (3-5 min). Do not let boil
- Once thick (spoon test), reduce heat to low and add cheese. Whisk until melted and smooth
- Add cooked pasta and stir until combined, add pasta water as desired
- Let cool for a 5 minutes until sauce is sticking to pasta
