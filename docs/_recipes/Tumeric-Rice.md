---
layout: recipe
yield: 8
time: .5
tags:
- mediterranean
ingredients:
- 2 Tbsp butter
- 1/2 medium onion, finely diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 2 cups basmati rice, rinsed and drained
- 3 cups chicken or vegetable stock
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp pepper
directions:
- Melt butter and saute onion and garlic for 1-2 minutes
- Add turmeric powder and combine, then mix in rice until lightly toasted
- Add stock, salt, and pepper. Cover, reduce heat to low and simmer for 15 min
- Remove from heat and rest for 5 minutes before fluffing
