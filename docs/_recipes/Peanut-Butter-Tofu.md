ingredients:
- 1 Tbsp sugar
- 1 Tbsp sesame oil
- 3 Tbsp light soy sauce
- 3 Tbsp peanut butter
- 1/2 tsp red chili flakes
- 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 5 Tbsp water
- 20 oz firm tofu
- oil for frying
directions:
- Mix sugar, sesame oil, soy sauce, peanut butter, chili flakes, garlic, and water in a bowl
- Cut tofu into cubes, fry in extra oil until golden and crispy
- Remove from pan, add sauce mixture and heat to simmer
- Toss tofu in the sauce
- Serve with rice or noodles
