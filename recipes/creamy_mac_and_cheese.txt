Creamy Mac and Cheese

Yield: 8

Time (hours): 0.25

Keywords: pasta, american

Recommended Sides:

Ingredients:
8oz uncooked pasta
2 Tbsp salted butter
2 Tbsp AP flour
1/2 tsp sea salt
1/4 tsp garlic powder
1 cup whole milk
1/4 cup sour cream or Greek yogurt
8 oz shredded cheddar cheese

Make:
1. Cook pasta according to package, save some pasta water. Drain and set aside.
2. Mix flour, salt, garlic powder in small bowl.
3. In medium saucepan, melt butter over medium heat.
4. Add flour mixture and whisk until smooth.
5. Add milk and whisk until smooth.
6. Add sour cream or yogurt, whisk until smooth.
7. Cook on medium-high heat until thickened (3-5 min). Do not let boil.
8. Once thick (spoon test), reduce heat to low and add cheese. Whisk until melted and smooth.
9. Add cooked pasta and stir until combined, add pasta water as desired.
10. Let cool for a 5 minutes until sauce is sticking to pasta.

Notes:
1. Reheat with a little extra milk to make creamy again.
