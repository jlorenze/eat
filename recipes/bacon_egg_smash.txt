Bacon Egg Smash

Yield: 4-6

Time (hours): 0.5

Keywords: breakfast, american

Recommended Sides: bread

Ingredients:
12 hard boiled eggs, chopped
1/2 cup greek yogurt or mayonnaise
1/4 cup cottage cheese
1/2 cup grated cheddar cheese
5 oz can green chilis
4-6 strips cooked bacon, crumbled
hot sauce or ketchup (optional)
salt
pepper

Make:
1. Combine eggs, yogurt, cheeses, chilis, bacon.
2. Add hot sauce or ketchup for extra kick, to taste.
3. Optionally, add salt and pepper to taste.
