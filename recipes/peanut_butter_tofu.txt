Peanut Butter Tofu

Yield: 4

Time (hours): 0.5

Keywords: asian, vegetarian

Recommended Sides: rice, vegetables

Ingredients:
1 Tbsp sugar
1 Tbsp sesame oil
3 Tbsp light soy sauce
3 Tbsp peanut butter
1/2 tsp red chili flakes
3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
5 Tbsp water
20 oz firm tofu
oil for frying

Make:
1. Mix sugar, sesame oil, soy sauce, peanut butter, chili flakes, garlic, and water in a bowl.
2. Cut tofu into cubes, fry in extra oil until golden and crispy.
3. Remove from pan, add sauce mixture and heat to simmer.
4. Toss tofu in the sauce.
5. Serve with rice or noodles.

Notes:
1. Try adding a little flour to the frying oil.
