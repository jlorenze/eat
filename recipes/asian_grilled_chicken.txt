Asian Grilled Chicken

Yield: 8

Time (hours): 0.5

Keywords: meat

Recommended Sides: rice, vegetables sweet and sour sauce

Ingredients:
3 lbs boneless chicken breast or thighs
4 Tbsp lemon juice
4 cloves garlic, minced
4 tsp fresh ginger, minced
4 green onions, minced
1/2 cup low sodium soy sauce
1/2 cup teriyaki sauce
2 tsp honey
4 tsp sesame oil
4 Tbsp toasted sesame seeds

Make:
1. If using chicken breast, slice in half into cutlets.
2. Combine all marinade ingredients (lemon juice - sesame oil), evenly cover chicken.
3. Marinate in refrigerator for 1-24 hours.
4. Grill 5-8 minutes per side until done.
5. Top with sesame seeds.
