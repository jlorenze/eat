Tumeric Rice

Yield: 8

Time (hours): .5

Keywords: mediterranean

Recommended Sides:

Ingredients:
2 Tbsp butter
1/2 medium onion, finely diced
4 cloves garlic, minced
1 tsp turmeric powder
2 cups basmati rice, rinsed and drained
3 cups chicken or vegetable stock
1/2 tsp salt
1/4 tsp pepper

Make:
1. Melt butter and saute onion and garlic for 1-2 minutes.
2. Add turmeric powder and combine, then mix in rice until lightly toasted.
3. Add stock, salt, and pepper. Cover, reduce heat to low and simmer for 15 min.
4. Remove from heat and rest for 5 minutes before fluffing.
