Focaccia

Yield: 1

Time (hours): 18

Keywords: bread

Recommended Sides:

Ingredients:
512g (4 cups) AP flour
10-15g (2-3 tsp) salt
8g (2 tsp) instant yeast
455g (2 cups) lukewarm water
4 Tbsp olive oil
flaky sea salt
rosemary, based, or other toppings

Make:
1. Whisk flour, salt, yeast. Add water. Mix until combined, cover and rest for 30 min.
2. Stretch and fold dough with wet hand.
3. Rub surface with some oil, cover and refrigerate 12 hours or up to 3 days.
4. Line 9x13 inch or cast iron with parchment or grease with some butter to help prevent sticking.
5. Pour 2 Tbsp oil into pan. Deflate dough gently and move to pan, then roll in oil to coat and form into rough ball.
6. Let rest for 3-4 hours (don't need to cover since oil protects).
7. Preheat oven to 425F.
8. Sprinkle toppings on dough, then pour 2 Tbsp oil and using fingers to dimple, stretching dough to fill pan.
9. Bake 25-30 min, until underside is golden and crisp.
10. Transfer from pan and cool on wire rack.
11. Store in airtight bag and store at room temperature. Reheat in oven at 350F.

Notes:
1. Cold dough from the rest in the fridge is key!
2. If you need to shorten time, let mixed dough rise at room temperature until doubled (1.5-2 hours), then proceed with the recipe. Note the second rise may take a bit longer.
