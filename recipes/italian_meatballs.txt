Italian Meatballs

Yield: 14

Time (hours): 2.5

Keywords: italian, meat

Recommended Sides: focaccia, bread, pasta

Ingredients:
2 lbs ground beef
2 large eggs
1 cup plain breadcrumbs
1/2 cup parmesan cheese
1/2 cup minced yellow onion
2 tsp salt
1 tsp black pepper
1 tsp Italian seasonings
1 tsp minced garlic
1/4 cup sliced basil
48 oz tomato sauce

Make:
1. Set oven rack to lower-middle, set to broil. Line baking sheet with foil and lightly grease with olive oil.
2. Mix beef, eggs, breadcrumbs, cheese, onion, salt, pepper, seasoning, garlic, and basil.
3. Make meatballs with 1/3 cup mixture and evenly space on sheet.
4. Broil meatballs 12-15 minutes, turning part way through, to brown.
5. Heat sauce in pot with lid, add broiled meatballs to sauce, cover and simmer on low heat for at least 1.5 hours. 

Notes:
1. Can also just bake meatballs at 375F for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway through.
2. Can also cook broiled meatballs in slow cooker with sauce added for 3 hours on high or 6 on low.
3. Can freeze cooked meatballs and sauce for 1 month.
