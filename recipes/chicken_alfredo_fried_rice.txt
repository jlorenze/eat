Chicken Alfredo Fried Rice

Yield: 8

Time (hours): 1

Keywords: italian, american, meat

Recommended Sides: vegetables

Ingredients:
3 lbs boneless skinless chicken breast
2.5 tsp salt
5 tsp italian herbs
4 tsp parsley
4 tsp garlic powder
4 tsp onion powder
3 tsp paprika
2.5 tsp chili flakes
3 Tbsp olive oil
2 cups uncooked basmati rice
2 Tbsp butter
8 tsp minced garlic
1/2 cup white onion, finely chopped
1/2 tsp salt
2 tsp italian herbs
1 tsp paprika
12 oz evaporated milk
4 oz parmesan cheese, grated
7 oz light cream cheese
8 oz jar sundried tomatoes, drained and chopped, reserve 1 Tbsp oil
1.5 cup white onion, chopped
handful parsley, chopped
1 tsp salt
3 tsp italian herbs
2 tsp paprika

Make:
1. Combine 2.5 tsp salt, 5 tsp italian herbs, 4 tsp parsley, 4 tsp garlic powder, 4 tsp onion powder, 3 tsp paprika, 2.5 tsp chili flakes, and 3 Tbsp olive oil.
2. Cut chicken breast into cutlets, add to marinade and set aside for 1-24 hours.
3. Grill chicken, 5-6 minutes per side, until done. Let rest covered in a bowl to collect any juices.
4. Rinse basmati rice until water runs clear, cook in a pot of boiling water, drain and set aside.
5. Melt 2 Tbsp butter over medium heat, add 8 tsp minced garlic and 1/2 cup onion, then cook 5 minutes.
6. Add 1/2 tsp salt, 2 tsp italian herbs, 1 tsp paprika, toast for 2 minutes. Then add milk, parmesan, and cream cheeses. Stir continuously until smooth and creamy. Set aside and keep warm.
7. In a skillet, heat reserved oil from tomatoes and reserved chicken juice, add 1.5 cup onion, sundried tomatoes, parsley, 1 tsp salt, 3 tsp italian herbs, and 2 tsp paprika.
8. Cook until onion is golden, then add cooked rice and mix until colored and fragrant.
9. Serve rice topped with chicken and sauce!
